# Loveliest — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information the **Loveliest** Discord bot (the "Bot") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Bot you agree to this policy and to our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

Questions or requests? Join our support server: https://discord.gg/fEqhUhNH2u

---

## 1. Who can use the Bot

Loveliest is only for people **18 or older**. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an under-18 user has registered, we will remove their data. If you believe a minor is using the Bot, tell us in the support server.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us when you register or edit your profile**

| Information | Notes |
|---|---|
| Discord user ID | Used to identify your account. |
| Name and display name | The name you enter during setup. |
| Date of birth and age | Used to confirm you are 18+. |
| Country | Entered during setup. |
| Location text | **Optional.** Only stored if you turn location on. |
| Email address | **Optional.** If you add one, we send a one-time code to verify it. |
| Bio and hobbies | Free text and interests you choose. |
| Profile photo and banner | Images you upload in your DMs with the Bot. |
| Gender | Used for matching preferences. |
| Terms acceptance | Whether you accepted the Terms of Service. |

**Information created when you use the Bot**

- **Matchmaking data:** your place in the queue (including which server/channel you ran the command in, your preferred gender, and chat or VC mode) and your matches (the user IDs involved, server and channel IDs used to relay messages, start and end times, and why a match ended).
- **Usage counters:** how many matches you have had and how many messages you have sent (counts only).
- **Social features:** blocks, marriage and family records, and related requests.
- **Safety records:** reports you submit or that are filed about you (including the reason text), and suspensions.
- **Private voice channels:** in VC mode, the Bot creates a temporary voice channel in our support server and stores its ID with your match so it can be deleted when the match ends.

**Messages.** Text messages you send to the Bot while matched are **relayed** to your match. The Bot needs Discord's Message Content intent to do this. The Bot does **not** save the content of your chats in its database; it only keeps counts. Keep in mind that your match will receive what you send, and Discord itself stores messages in your DMs under Discord's own policies.

**Voice.** The Bot does not join, listen to, or record voice channels. Voice audio is handled by Discord.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and show your profile and run matchmaking.
- To relay messages between matched users and create private voice channels.
- To verify your age and (if you add it) your email address.
- To prevent abuse, handle reports, and enforce our Terms (including suspensions and bans).
- To keep the Bot running, fix bugs, and understand overall usage.

We do **not** sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. What other users can see

Your match does not see your Discord account in text chat, because messages go through the Bot. Other users may see the profile details you choose to share, such as your display name, bio, hobbies, photos, banner, approximate location, and age. **Your email address is never shown to other users.** In voice mode, your match can see your Discord display name and avatar once you join the voice channel. Be careful about what you share in your profile and in chat.

## 5. Who we share information with

- **Discord.** The Bot runs on Discord, and Discord processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Email provider.** If you add an email address, it is sent to our email service only to deliver your verification code.
- **Hosting.** Data is stored on the server where the Bot is hosted, which is run by the Bot's operators.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, or if needed to protect users from serious harm, for example in cases of threats, exploitation, or illegal activity.

We do not share your information with any other third parties.

## 6. Storage and retention

Your data is stored in a database and an image folder on the Bot's server. We keep your profile and related data **until you delete it** with `/deleteprofile` (or ask us to). When a match ends, its private voice channel is deleted. Some records may be kept after deletion where reasonably necessary to prevent abuse, such as reports and suspensions, or to meet legal obligations. Backups may keep deleted data for a short period before they are overwritten.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **View or edit:** use `/editprofile` to change your information.
- **Delete:** use `/deleteprofile` to permanently delete your profile and its data.
- **Leave a match, block, or report:** use `/unmatch`, block a user, or report them at any time.
- **Withdraw consent:** stop using the Bot and delete your profile.
- **Other requests:** depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your data, or to object to its use. Ask us in the support server and we will respond within a reasonable time.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, such as limiting who can access the server and using verification codes for email. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not share sensitive information such as passwords or financial details with other users.

## 9. International users

The Bot is hosted in one location but used worldwide. By using it, you understand your information may be processed in a country other than your own.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will try to announce them in the support server or through the Bot. Continuing to use the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.
