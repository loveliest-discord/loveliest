Loveliest lets you create a profile, join a global matchmaking queue with `/matchup`, and chat anonymously with another user. Matched users talk through the Bot's direct messages, and if both choose **VC** mode, in a private voice channel created in our support server. You can end a match at any time with `/unmatch`, and you can block and report users.

The Bot is provided for social and entertainment purposes. It is not a dating guarantee, and we do not promise you will be matched with anyone.
